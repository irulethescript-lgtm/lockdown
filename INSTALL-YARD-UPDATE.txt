LOCKDOWN — TRAVEL YARD UPDATE FOR YOUR EXISTING GAME FOLDER

This download is for the Game folder where your game already works.
It contains ALL game code, templates, scripts and styles, including routes/auth.js,
the new Travel Yard and all previous gameplay updates. It reuses the artwork
already in your Game/public folder. Do not install this into an empty folder.

1. Stop the game: press Ctrl+C in its Command Prompt window.
2. Back up your existing Game folder.
3. Right-click lockdown-yard-update.zip and choose Extract All.
   Set the destination to your existing game folder:

     C:\Users\User\Desktop\Game

   Merge the folders and replace matching files when Windows asks.
   Keep your existing db folder, public artwork and other files.
   Do NOT delete the Game folder before extracting.
   No player database or node_modules folder is included in the ZIP.

4. Check that these files are directly inside your Game folder:

     Game\server.js
     Game\routes\auth.js
     Game\middleware\travel.js
     Game\public\js\travel.js
     Game\views\yard.ejs

   They must not be nested inside Game\lockdown-yard-update\.

5. In Command Prompt run:

     cd /d C:\Users\User\Desktop\Game
     npm ci
     npm start

   Node.js22.20.0 is supported. npm ci installs the correct dependencies
   for Windows and does not delete your db folder.

6. Open http://localhost:3000 and press Ctrl+F5 to refresh scripts and styles.
   Click Travel Yard in the sidebar, or visit:

     http://localhost:3000/prison/yard

HOW THE NEW YARD WORKS
- Pay a round-trip fare and visit another prison (2–12 minutes each way).
- The timer keeps running when you close the browser or restart the game.
- Buy exclusive goods after arrival, pack your bag and click Return.
- Cargo reaches your inventory automatically when you get home.
- Sell to the collector or trade on Open Market > Collectibles.
- Discover 10 unique goods for a one-time $1,500 and 5 reputation reward.
- Bigger bags unlock after 3 and 10 completed trips, plus their upgrade fees.
- Your home prison, bank, job and cell stay in place.
- Training, crimes and home trading wait until you return.
- Return travel is already paid, even if your cash and bag are empty.

Your existing accounts and progress are kept in your existing db folder.
New tables are added automatically when the updated game starts.
If you get an error, copy the exact terminal message into this conversation.
